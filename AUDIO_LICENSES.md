# Audio asset and licensing record

## Copyright policy

Mini ZekaCity must use only audio that the project team has the right to use and redistribute. Do not add commercial recordings, ripped game audio, unverified "royalty-free" downloads, or assets whose license cannot be confirmed.

## Current implementation

- `scripts/AudioManager.gd` generates the ambient music and all sound effects mathematically at runtime.
- No third-party audio recordings, samples, loops, or sound-effect files are bundled.
- The melodies, tones, envelopes, and synthesis implementation in this file were created specifically for Mini ZekaCity for this project.
- Since the current audio is generated by the project's own source code and does not incorporate third-party recordings or samples, there are no external audio asset licenses or attribution requirements for this implementation.

## Future additions

Before adding any external audio file, record its exact source, creator, license name/version, license URL, attribution text (if required), and any redistribution or commercial-use restrictions here. Prefer CC0 1.0 Universal or verified public-domain material when using third-party assets. "Free download" or "royalty-free" alone is not sufficient proof of permission.
